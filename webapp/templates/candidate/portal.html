Submission guide

Your zip must contain these files at the top level (the starter template has them all):

FileWhat it does
pipeline.tomlpython = "3.12" (3.11 to 3.13), train = "python train.py", predict = "python predict.py". Notebooks can be run with papermill.
requirements.txtEvery package you import, pinned: scikit-learn==1.5.2. No URLs or index options.
DECISIONS.mdA line claimed_metric: roc_auc = 0.812, a line seed: 42, how you validated, and which AI tools you used.
README.mdHow your pipeline works.

When your code runs, these environment variables are set:

  • Training: PP_DATA_DIR (folder with train.csv), PP_SEED (use it for every random step), and PP_OUTPUT_DIR (write your model and a metrics.json such as {"roc_auc": 0.812}).
  • Prediction: PP_MODEL_DIR (the folder training wrote to), PP_INPUT (a CSV like train.csv without the target) and PP_PRED_OUT (write a CSV with two columns: row_id and your predicted probability).

There is no internet access while your code runs, and each run is limited in time and memory. Files outside your zip do not exist.

For candidates

If an employer has sent you a PipelineProof task

Your work is judged on whether the result you report holds up, not on how your code looks. Here is exactly what happens to it.

What we do with your submission

  1. We check it follows the submission format and run it on a small sample, so you can fix problems before you submit.
  2. After you submit, we run your training four times in a sealed environment with no internet: twice with your seed and twice with others.
  3. Your model predicts customers that are not in the training data. We compare that score with the one you reported.
  4. We record how your code splits and fits the data, to see whether any test or future information reached training.
  5. A person at the employer reads the report and makes the decision. The software never rejects anyone on its own.

Your rights

  • After the employer has reviewed your work, they can release feedback to you, including the evidence about your submission.
  • You can contest the result from your candidate page. A person at the employer will read it and reply.
  • Your data is kept only for the employer's retention period and then deleted. The employer is responsible for your data and their privacy notice applies.

How to prepare

  • Start from the starter template on your candidate page; it already reads and writes the right files.
  • Pin every package in requirements.txt to an exact version, and include everything you import.
  • Read your random seed from the PP_SEED environment variable so your result can be reproduced.
  • Your code runs without internet access and only with the files you submit. Do not include the data; it is provided when your code runs.
  • Report the score you actually measured. A careful 0.80 that holds up on new customers is a better result than a 0.97 that does not.

AI assistants

Unless the employer says otherwise, you may use any tools, including AI assistants. Say which you used in DECISIONS.md. You are judged on the result and the decisions you made, whoever typed the code.
